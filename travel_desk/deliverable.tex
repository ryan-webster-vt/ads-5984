\section{Question 2}\label{question-2}

\subsection{List what each of the four agents is shown and what it is
not shown. Point to the exact lines that decide this, and explain what
would go wrong --- in tool-permission terms and in context terms --- if
one agent held both inventories and both booking
tools.}\label{list-what-each-of-the-four-agents-is-shown-and-what-it-is-not-shown.-point-to-the-exact-lines-that-decide-this-and-explain-what-would-go-wrong-in-tool-permission-terms-and-in-context-terms-if-one-agent-held-both-inventories-and-both-booking-tools.}

Router: start and destination, depart and return dates, other notes and
routing instructions. Not shown: flight/hotel inventories, log, budget.
Evidence is shown in def router on line 219.

Flight Desk: Shown: Flight notes, destination, current held flight, and
the over-budget amount while needing to trade down. Not shown:
Inventory, Anything hotel related, resolver's reasoning, budget when not
needed. Evidence is shown in def desk on 246.

Hotel Desk: Hotel notes, hotel at destination, current held hotel, and
the over-budge amount while needing to trade down. Not shown: Inventory,
Anything flight related, resolver's reasoning, budget when not needed.
Evidence is shown in def hotel on 246 when kind == hotel

Resolver: Shown: notes, budget, held total, the two held bookings, log
history. Not shown: Inventories, origin, destination, dates, pending,
and the rest of the state. Evidence shown on line 303, def
make\_resolver

The reason why we separate duties is due to wanting least privilege
amongst tool calls and inventory access and the need to keep context
focused and short to reduce the risk of hallucinations.

\section{Question 3}\label{question-3}

\subsection{The coordinator is the only node that writes flight, hotel,
total and status. Which Module 12 anti-pattern does that single-writer
rule prevent, and what concrete bad bill could you get if the flight
desk could write flight directly while the hotel quote stayed as it
was?}\label{the-coordinator-is-the-only-node-that-writes-flight-hotel-total-and-status.-which-module-12-anti-pattern-does-that-single-writer-rule-prevent-and-what-concrete-bad-bill-could-you-get-if-the-flight-desk-could-write-flight-directly-while-the-hotel-quote-stayed-as-it-was}

The Module 12 anti-pattern here would be shared mutable states without
arbitration. Also, if the flight desk could write flight directly while
the hotel quote stayed, the flight desk could change the flight while
keeping the stale hotel quote, possibly overpaying for an unused hotel.

\section{Step 8 Reflection}\label{step-8-reflection}

Removing this guardrail gets rid of the no human-in-the-loop
anti-pattern and allows the application to throw a GraphRecrusionError
when there are no possible answers. This applies to not only this
example, but any ReAct loop that may not have any possible answers and
requires HITL. Also, letting a model determine when to stop is a bad
design as in this example, the prompt encourages to model to keep trying
the hardest it can.
