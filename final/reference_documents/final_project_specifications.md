### **35.1 Overview**

For the final project, you must choose one of the ideas below (or a project idea the instructor has approved) and deliver a fully-functional, multi-agent solution with a streamlit front-end.

There are no in-class final presentations. You present your project at the **Agentic AI Showcase** (Thursday, December 3, 5–7 PM, New Classroom Building 220): you staff a table, demo your system live, and walk visitors and the instructor through it. Be ready to cover these required sections at your table:

High-level overview of the problem we are solving with this solution Design overview that includes "why does this solution require a multi-agent design" UML class diagrams for agents and sequence diagrams showing the main agentic loop (and how agents interact over time) Python source code with descriptive in-line comments

Also submit on Canvas, by the start of the showcase: a .zip of your project source with a README that has run instructions (list the expected .env keys, but do **not** include your .env ), and a PDF of your UML class and sequence diagrams.

Before you build, complete the [Requirements](https://ads1.datasci.vt.edu/ads_5984/assignments/final_project_reqs_and_arch.html) & Architecture write-up — a separate 500-point exercise, due at the **end of W11 (Fri Nov 6)**, that mirrors the mid-term's and opens with an introduction explaining what you are trying to accomplish with your project. **No AI assistance on that document.**

Your handout infographic and looping video for the showcase are a separate assignment, Showcase [Infographic](https://ads1.datasci.vt.edu/ads_5984/assignments/showcase_infographic_video.html) & [Video](https://ads1.datasci.vt.edu/ads_5984/assignments/showcase_infographic_video.html), due **Friday, November 20**.

### **35.2 Use of OpenCode or ClaudeCode**

Use of OpenCode or ClaudeCode is highly encouraged, however, you are 100% responsible for the veracity of your code and meeting all the requirements above. You can't say "the AI generated that." You are responsible. Also, you will be asked complex questions about how your code works (at your showcase table), so be ready to explain what is happening in your code in detail.
