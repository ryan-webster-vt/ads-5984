# Automotive market platform

Cockpit AV platforms, AI cockpit and infotainment solutions, and associated development agreements under the **DRIVE** brand (10-K FY2022).

## FY2022 (10-K FY2022)

- Revenue **+6% YoY** — self-driving and AI cockpit solutions, offset by a decline in legacy cockpit revenue.
- **Orin** SoC began shipping in FY2022 (deep neural network processing for DRIVE AutoPilot).
- Unveiled **DRIVE Atlan**, the next-generation SoC.
- Announced design wins: Mercedes-Benz (AI cockpit, EQS sedan), Volvo (autonomous driving computer, XC90 from 2022), EV makers (R-Auto, IM Motors, NIO, Faraday Future, VinFast, Xpeng), robotaxis (Cruise, Amazon Zoox, Pony.ai, AutoX), autonomous trucking (Embark, Kodiak Robotics, Plus), multi-year Jaguar Land Rover partnership; Desay, Flex, Quanta, Valeo, ZF building on DRIVE Hyperion.
- Platform: **DRIVE Hyperion** (DRIVE AGX compute + reference sensor set + DRIVE Software), DRIVE Chauffeur (AV), DRIVE Concierge (in-cabin AI), **DRIVE Constellation** simulator running DRIVE Sim; over-the-air software-defined architecture; several hundred ecosystem partners.

## FY2023 (10-K FY2023)

- Revenue **$903M, +60% YoY** — growth in self-driving solutions, computing solutions for **electric-vehicle makers**, AI cockpit sales, and automotive development arrangements.
- **DRIVE Orin** autonomous-vehicle SoC entered production; next-generation **DRIVE Thor** introduced (10-K FY2023). (Atlan, unveiled FY2022, is not mentioned in the FY2023 filing's narrative.)

## FY2024–FY2026 (10-K FY2024, FY2025, FY2026)

- **FY2024: $1.091B, +21%** — design wins and adoption with **BYD, XPENG, GWM, Li Auto, ZEEKR and Xiaomi**; **MediaTek partnership** (mainstream automotive SoCs integrating NVIDIA GPU chiplet IP); Foxconn collaboration; DRIVE Hyperion.
- **FY2025: $1.694B, +55%** — "driven by sales of our self-driving platforms."
- **FY2026: +39%** (dollar figure not recoverable) — "continued adoption of our self-driving platforms" (10-K FY2026); **DRIVE AGX / DRIVE OS / DRIVE Hyperion** platform.
- Note: in FY2026 NVIDIA moved **Automotive platforms and autonomous/electric-vehicle solutions, including software,** into the **Compute & Networking** segment definition, while **automotive platforms for infotainment** remain in Graphics — a re-cut of the segment definitions (see [Reportable segments](reportable-segments.md)).

## Reporting note

Automotive is split across segments: **infotainment platforms** sit in [Graphics](reportable-segments.md); **AI Cockpit and autonomous-driving development agreements** sit in Compute & Networking (10-K FY2022). Revenue is largely license/development arrangements — recognized over the development period.
