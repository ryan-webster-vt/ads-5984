# Gaming market platform

NVIDIA's largest market platform through FY2022: GeForce RTX/GTX GPUs for desktop and laptop PCs, GeForce NOW cloud gaming, SHIELD, and platforms for console devices (10-K FY2022). FY2023: overtaken by [Data Center](data-center.md) as the largest platform.

## FY2022 (10-K FY2022)

- Revenue **+61% YoY**, driven by the ramp of **NVIDIA Ampere architecture RTX 30 Series** GPUs, new ray-tracing games, esports/content-creation/streaming popularity, work-from-home system demand, and the ability of end users to mine [cryptocurrency](cryptocurrency-exposure.md) with GeForce GPUs.
- Nearly all desktop Ampere GeForce shipments from Q2 FY2022 were **LHR (Lite Hash Rate)** cards with limited Ethereum mining capability, to steer supply to gamers.
- RTX ecosystem expanded to **240+ games and applications**; DLSS integration planned for the Unity game engine; new GeForce NOW membership tier.
- China risk: government restrictions on gaming duration and access cited as a risk to the Gaming business.

## FY2023 (10-K FY2023)

- Revenue **$9.07B, −27% YoY** — the correction FY2022's risk factors warned about. Rapid macro deterioration + COVID disruptions in China left **excess channel inventory**; NVIDIA ran pricing programs for channel partners and deliberately **undershipped GPU supply** to drain the channel, making "meaningful progress" toward lower channel inventory by fiscal year-end.
- Excess Ampere-architecture Gaming product was a major component of the **$2.17B inventory charges** (particularly vs demand expectations in China); GeForce margin compression contributed to the Graphics segment gross-margin decline.
- Product cycle continued despite the downturn: **Ada Lovelace architecture** launched — GeForce RTX 4090, RTX 4080, RTX 4070 Ti desktop GPUs; laptop GPUs in 170+ designs; **DLSS 3** in 50+ games/apps; GeForce RTX 4080-class performance added to GeForce NOW Ultimate tier.

## FY2024–FY2026 (10-K FY2024, FY2025, FY2026)

- **FY2024: $10.447B, +15%** — recovery "driven by higher sell-in to partners following the normalization of channel inventory levels." Product cycle: GeForce RTX 4060/4070 (Ada Lovelace), RTX 40-Series SUPER, DLSS 3.5 Ray Reconstruction, TensorRT-LLM for Windows; milestone of **500 RTX AI games/apps** and >100M AI-capable PCs.
- **FY2025: $11.350B, +9%** — "driven by sales of our GeForce RTX 40 Series GPUs"; **Blackwell GeForce RTX 50 Series** launched (neural graphics, up to 2x performance).
- **FY2026: +41%** (dollar figure not recoverable) — "driven by strong Blackwell demand," but NVIDIA expects **supply constraints to be a headwind to Gaming in Q1 FY2027 and beyond** (10-K FY2026).
- Gaming is now a small share of total revenue as Data Center dominates, but remains the anchor of the [Graphics segment](reportable-segments.md) and the consumer/CUDA installed base. China's gaming-duration restrictions remain a cited risk.

## Product technology

- **NVIDIA RTX**: real-time ray tracing + **DLSS** (deep learning super sampling) AI upscaling.
- Seasonality: consumer products typically stronger in the second half of the fiscal year — but FY2022 demand exceeded supply and broke seasonal patterns.
