# Supply chain and manufacturing

NVIDIA is **fabless**: wafer fabrication, assembly, test and packaging are all outsourced; it focuses on design, quality assurance and support (10-K FY2022).

## Suppliers (10-K FY2022)

- **Foundries**: Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), Samsung Electronics.
- **Assembly/test/packaging subcontractors**: Amkor, King Yuan Electronics (KYEC), Omni Logistics, Siliconware Precision Industries (SPIL), Wistron.
- **Contract manufacturers** (adapter cards, switch systems): Flex, Jabil, Universal Scientific Industrial (USI); Fabrinet (cables).
- **Substrates**: Ibiden, Kinsus, Unimicron. **Memory**: Micron, Samsung Semiconductor, SK Hynix.
- Distribution from third-party warehouses in Hong Kong, Israel, and the US.

## FY2022: supply-constrained (10-K FY2022)

- Demand exceeded supply in several areas; constraints expected to persist into **1H FY2023**, primarily Gaming and Networking.
- Manufacturing lead times **12+ months**; NVIDIA moved from ~6-month to ≥12-month advance ordering, placing **non-cancellable orders**, paying premiums and deposits.
- **$1.87B prepayments** for long-term supply agreements; **$1.58B** remaining unpaid under those agreements; total inventory purchase + long-term supply obligations **$9.00B** plus $1.30B other purchase obligations = **$10.30B** total commitments (mostly due FY2023: $9.3B). A further $670M/9-year supply agreement signed March 2022.
- Inventory provisions rose to **$354M** (FY2022) from $116M (FY2021); net gross-margin impact −0.9%.
- Risk acknowledged: long-lead-time orders placed before product designs are finalized could trigger excess-inventory charges if demand falls or is served by competitors.

## FY2024 (10-K FY2024)

- Inventory purchase + long-term supply/capacity obligations **$16.1B**; total purchase commitments **$20.7B** incl. **$3.5B multi-year cloud agreements**. Prepaid supply/capacity $2.458B long-term + $2.5B short-term.
- Suppliers named: TSMC and Samsung (wafers); Micron, SK Hynix, Samsung (memory); **CoWoS** packaging; Hon Hai (Foxconn), Wistron, Fabrinet (assembly/test/packaging); supply chain "concentrated in the Asia-Pacific region"; >12-month lead times persisted.
- Inventory $5.3B; inventory provisions $774M; total inventory + excess-purchase-obligation provisions **$2.2B**; provision releases $540M.

## FY2025 (10-K FY2025)

- Obligations jump to **$30.8B** inventory/supply/capacity + $14.3B other (incl. **$10.9B cloud agreements**); total purchase commitments **$45.1B**. Prepaid supply/capacity $3.3B current + $1.747B long-term.
- Inventory $10.08B; inventory provisions **$1.6B**; total provisions **$3.7B**. NVIDIA continues to pay premiums, provide deposits and sign long-term capacity commitments; risk "has increased and may continue to increase."
- **Tariffs** first appear as a recurring cost/risk item. Critical audit matter covers inventory/excess-commitment valuation.

## FY2026 (10-K FY2026)

- Obligations reach **$95.2B**, "substantially all" payable through FY2027; cloud-service commitments **$27B**; investment commitments $11.4B; other $3.4B. Inventory $21.4B; inventory provisions **$4.0B**; total provisions **$7.2B** incl. **$4.5B for H20** (see [Export controls](export-controls.md)).
- Supply chain still "mainly concentrated in Asia," but NVIDIA is **expanding into the US and Latin America** and investing in domestic manufacturing.
- NVIDIA expects **supply constraints to be a headwind to Gaming** in Q1 FY2027 and beyond. Inventory/excess-commitment valuation remains the critical audit matter.
- Cross-filing pattern to note: commitments grew ~9x from FY2022 to FY2026, and excess-inventory charges recur (FY2023 Ampere, FY2025 Blackwell yields, FY2026 H20), so the [risk-factor](risk-factors.md) demand-estimation warning has repeatedly materialized.
