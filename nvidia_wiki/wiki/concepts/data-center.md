# Data Center market platform

Accelerates AI (deep learning, machine learning), data analytics, graphics and scientific computing across hyperscale, cloud, enterprise, public sector and edge data centers (10-K FY2022). As of FY2023, the largest market platform.

## FY2022 (10-K FY2022)

- Revenue **+58% YoY**, primarily NVIDIA Ampere architecture GPU sales for both **training and inference** to hyperscale customers and vertical industries — cloud computing, natural language processing, deep recommender models.
- First full year of [Mellanox](../entities/mellanox.md) networking revenue (acquired April 2020) also contributed.
- Launched A30 and A10 GPUs for mainstream AI/analytics/graphics; NVIDIA-Certified Systems with server OEMs; **NVIDIA AI Enterprise** software suite launched; Base Command and Fleet Command SaaS unveiled; **Grace CPU** (Arm-based data center CPU) unveiled; Earth-2 climate AI supercomputer announced; Quantum-2 400Gbps InfiniBand networking platform (Nov 2021 GTC).

## FY2023 (10-K FY2023)

- Revenue **$15.01B, +41% YoY** — led by hyperscale customers, plus purchases by several CSP partners to support the new [NVIDIA AI cloud services](ai-cloud-services.md) and NVIDIA's own R&D. Became NVIDIA's largest market platform, overtaking [Gaming](gaming.md).
- **Hopper GPU architecture** announced and first products began ramping, including the **H100 Tensor Core GPU**; **OVX server** reference design launched for digital twins and Omniverse applications.
- Networking: **Spectrum-4** end-to-end 400Gbps Ethernet platform announced; **Quantum-2** began shipping December 2022.
- Cloud AI services: completed **NeMo LLM** and **BioNeMo LLM** large language models.
- Offset within the year: excess Ampere Data Center product contributed to the $2.17B inventory charges (China demand expectations); $122M Q2 warranty charge for a third-party component defect in certain Data Center products (~$70M recovered in Q3).
- Headwind disclosed: Q3 FY2023 [US export controls](export-controls.md) on A100/H100/DGX/A100X to China (incl. HK/Macau) and Russia; A800 offered as a China alternative.

## FY2024 (10-K FY2024) — generative-AI step change

- Revenue **$47.525B, +217%** (from $15.005B): compute +244%, networking +133% on Hopper GPU shipments for training/inference, recommendation engines and generative AI. ~40% of Data Center revenue was AI inference; in Q4 large cloud providers were more than half of Data Center revenue.
- Drivers: Hopper/H100 ramp; **Grace CPU and Grace Hopper Superchips** began shipping Q3 FY2024; **Spectrum-X** Ethernet-for-AI platform announced; DGX Cloud + AI Foundations; NVIDIA AI Enterprise with NeMo.
- Headwinds: October 2023 [US export controls](export-controls.md) cut China to 14% of Data Center revenue (from 19%); China remained "competitive."

## FY2025 (10-K FY2025) — another doubling

- Revenue **$115.186B, +142%**: compute **$102.196B (+162%)**, networking **$12.990B (+51%**, Ethernet for AI / Spectrum-X). Over 88% of total company revenue.
- **Blackwell launched** (full data-center-scale platform: GPUs, CPUs, DPUs, interconnects, switches, systems, adapters) and **began shipping production systems in Q4 FY2025** (GB200 NVL72/NVL36, B200); Hopper demand drove the full year. A new **annual architecture cadence** was introduced.
- Q2 FY2025 gross margin was hurt by inventory provisions for **low-yielding Blackwell material**; total inventory/purchase-obligation provisions $3.7B.
- [Export controls](export-controls.md) escalate: Jan 2025 **AI Diffusion IFR** proposes worldwide licensing; restricted-product licenses still not granted for China.

## FY2026 (10-K FY2026) — scale with margin mix shift

- Revenue **+68%** (dollar figure not recoverable from this conversion): compute +59%, networking **+142%** on **NVLink compute fabric for GB200/GB300** plus Ethernet/InfiniBand growth.
- **Blackwell Ultra / GB300** shipped from Q2 FY2026; **Rubin** unveiled for 2H FY2027 production (~10x lower cost per token than Blackwell). **NVLink Fusion** lets hyperscalers integrate custom CPUs/XPUs (see [Blackwell and Rubin](blackwell-and-rubin.md)).
- Gross margin compressed to 71.1% as the model shifted from Hopper HGX systems to Blackwell full-scale data-center solutions, plus a **$4.5B H20 charge** ([export controls](export-controls.md)).
- **China:** NVIDIA states it is "effectively foreclosed from competing in China's data center computing/compute market," benefiting competitors' ecosystems.

## Platform composition (10-K FY2022)

- Energy-efficient GPUs, BlueField **DPUs** (with DOCA software), interconnects and systems; CUDA + CUDA-X libraries; NGC software registry.
- System families: **DGX** (AI supercomputer), **HGX** (hyperscale), **EGX** (enterprise/edge), **AGX** (autonomous machines).
- Enterprise partners: Amazon, Alphabet, IBM, Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, VMware; every major server maker (Cisco, Dell, HPE, Hitachi, Inspur, Lenovo); every major cloud.
- End uses: consumer internet services (search, recommendations, translation), enterprise AI (chatbots, fraud detection), healthcare imaging/drug discovery, HPC (climate, genomics).
