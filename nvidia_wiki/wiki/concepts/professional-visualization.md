# Professional Visualization market platform

Serves designers, engineers and content creators working with ISVs optimized for NVIDIA GPUs: design & manufacturing (CAD, architecture, aerospace), digital content creation (video, film VFX, broadcast) (10-K FY2022).

## FY2022 (10-K FY2022)

- Revenue **+100% YoY** — ramp of NVIDIA Ampere architecture products and strong workstation demand as enterprises supported **hybrid work**, plus growth in 3D design, AI and rendering workloads.
- Unveiled NVIDIA RTX GPUs for next-generation notebook and desktop workstations.
- **Omniverse Enterprise** reached general availability (Nov 2021 GTC): real-time 3D design collaboration, digital twins and virtual worlds — subscription for enterprise, free for individuals. NVIDIA positions Omniverse as the platform for physically-based rendering and simulation (virtual showrooms, surgical training, architectural walkthroughs).

## FY2023 (10-K FY2023)

- Revenue **$1.54B, −27% YoY** — same channel-correction dynamic as [Gaming](gaming.md): rapid change in economic conditions left **excess inventory with OEM partners**; NVIDIA cut sell-in to reduce it.
- Product activity continued: added new Ampere-architecture RTX GPUs for workstations; announced **Omniverse Avatar Cloud Engine** and **Omniverse Cloud**; major update to Omniverse Enterprise. FY2023 Item 1 frames Omniverse as the platform for **metaverse** and 3D-internet applications.

## FY2024–FY2026 (10-K FY2024, FY2025, FY2026)

- **FY2024: $1.553B, +1%** — flat; RTX Ada Lovelace workstation GPUs and **Omniverse Cloud** (fully managed service on Microsoft Azure) announced.
- **FY2025: $1.878B, +21%** — RTX PRO GPUs and Omniverse subscription growth.
- **FY2026: +70%** (dollar figure not recoverable) — "driven by exceptional demand for Blackwell as well as the launch of our new **DGX Spark**" (10-K FY2026). This makes FY2026 ProViz the fastest-growing non-Data-Center platform.

## Notes

- Shares RTX ray-tracing technology with [Gaming](gaming.md); workstation GPUs (Quadro/NVIDIA RTX) and vGPU and Omniverse are reported in the [Graphics segment](reportable-segments.md).
- Earth-2 (climate digital twin) is planned to be built on Omniverse.
