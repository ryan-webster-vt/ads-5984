# Reportable segments

As of the FY2022 10-K, NVIDIA reports **two operating segments** (Note 17, 10-K FY2022). The CODM is CEO [Jensen Huang](../entities/jensen-huang.md). There is no intersegment revenue; the CODM does not review assets by segment.

## Graphics

- GeForce GPUs for gaming and PCs; GeForce NOW game streaming; solutions for gaming platforms
- Quadro/NVIDIA RTX GPUs for enterprise workstation graphics
- vGPU software for cloud-based visual and virtual computing
- Automotive platforms for **infotainment** systems
- Omniverse software for 3D design and virtual worlds

## Compute & Networking

- Data Center platforms and systems for AI, HPC, accelerated computing
- [Mellanox](../entities/mellanox.md) networking and interconnect
- Automotive **AI Cockpit**, autonomous-driving development agreements, AV solutions
- CMP (cryptocurrency mining processors)
- Jetson for robotics/embedded
- NVIDIA AI Enterprise and other software

## All Other

Expenses not assigned to either segment: stock-based compensation, corporate infrastructure and support, acquisition-related costs, IP-related costs, and non-recurring enterprise-level charges.

## Segment detail (10-K FY2022)

- Both segments grew revenue ~61% in FY2022 vs FY2021.
- Goodwill allocated: Graphics $361M; Compute & Networking $3.99B.
- Graphics gross margin up YoY on richer GeForce mix; Compute & Networking gross margin down on mix shift and lower Automotive contribution.
- Segment reporting is a distinct lens from the four **market platforms** ([Gaming](gaming.md), [Data Center](data-center.md), [Professional Visualization](professional-visualization.md), [Automotive](automotive.md)) + OEM & Other, which cut across segments.

## FY2024–FY2026 (10-K FY2024, FY2025, FY2026)

The **two-segment structure is unchanged** across all filings in this wiki, but details evolved:

- **FY2024:** still "Compute & Networking" and "Graphics." Compute grew 266% (Hopper for LLM training/inference) and networking +133%. Goodwill: Compute & Networking $4.1B; Graphics $370M. "All Other" operating loss $(4,890)M vs $(5,411)M FY2023 (the drop was mainly the absence of the $1.353B Arm charge, offset by +$839M SBC).
- **FY2025:** same two segments. NVIDIA **adopted the FASB standard requiring disclosure of significant segment expenses** (ASU 2023-07), adding segment depreciation/amortization detail: Compute & Networking $732M, Graphics $372M. Goodwill: $4.8B / $370M. "All Other" $(6,507)M.
- **FY2026 — segment definitions NARROWED (flag):** the Graphics segment is now defined as only "GeForce GPUs for gaming and PCs, and Quadro/NVIDIA RTX GPUs for enterprise workstation graphics," while Compute & Networking adds "AI solutions and software" and automotive/AEV solutions including software. Prior-year descriptions had put **automotive infotainment and Omniverse in Graphics** and **Jetson/DGX Cloud in Compute & Networking** (10-K FY2025 vs FY2026). Still two reportable segments; no "All Other" segment — unallocated costs are listed directly.
- **No segment gross margin** is reported in any year; the CODM measures segment **revenue and segment operating income**. Data Center dwarfs the rest, so segment results now essentially track Compute & Networking.
- **Goodwill:** Graphics held at $370M throughout; Compute & Networking rises $4.0B (FY2023) → $4.1B (FY2024) → $4.8B (FY2025) → **$20.5B (FY2026)** on the [Groq](../entities/groq.md) license.

> Note: segment revenue/operating-income tables and most balance-sheet tables appear as `[TABLE]` placeholders in the raw markdown conversions, so segment dollar figures are generally not recoverable; where known they are cited with their filing year.
