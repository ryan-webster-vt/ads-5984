# NVIDIA AI cloud services

First appears in the FY2023 10-K: NVIDIA moves beyond selling chips and systems into offering **AI services directly and through partners** — a shift toward recurring, cloud-delivered revenue layered on the [accelerated computing](accelerated-computing.md) platform (10-K FY2023).

## What was announced (10-K FY2023)

- **NVIDIA DGX Cloud** — cloud-based infrastructure and software for training AI models, co-located with partner cloud service providers.
- **Customizable pretrained AI models** — NVIDIA completed two large language models for cloud AI services: **NVIDIA NeMo LLM** and **NVIDIA BioNeMo LLM** (biology/molecular).
- Offered directly to enterprise customers and through NVIDIA's partner network; leading CSPs host the services in their data centers.
- **Multi-year cloud service agreements with several CSP partners signed in 2H FY2023** to support the offerings and NVIDIA's own R&D; $2.23B of multi-year cloud service commitments sit inside purchase obligations (Note 13).
- Related cloud services revenue model (Note 1): subscription (recognized ratably) or subscription + usage (recognized as usage occurs); deferred revenue includes cloud-services customer advances.

## Why it matters

- Extends NVIDIA's business model up the stack (hardware → systems → software → services), alongside standalone software (NVIDIA AI Enterprise, Omniverse, DRIVE).
- Risk factors flag that the services "may not be successful and will take time, resources and investment" — new business-model execution risk (10-K FY2023).
- Contextual frame: FY2023 Item 1 centers on **generative AI** and LLMs as the next platform shift; hyperscale/CSP demand for AI training drove [Data Center](data-center.md) revenue +41%.

## FY2024–FY2026 (10-K FY2024, FY2025, FY2026)

- **FY2024:** DGX Cloud launched as "an AI-training-as-a-service platform" with cloud infrastructure, customizable pretrained models and NVIDIA experts, hosted by leading CSPs; **AI Foundations** to help businesses build custom LLMs/generative models.
- **FY2025:** NVIDIA AI Enterprise expanded to include **NIM** (inference microservices, "2.5x increase in token throughput"), **NeMo** and **AI Blueprints**; NVIDIA continued entering multi-year cloud service agreements.
- **FY2026:** cloud-service commitments reached **$27B**; NVIDIA notes it "may not be able to establish market share sufficient to achieve the scale necessary." An unnamed "AI research and deployment company" is estimated to contribute meaningfully to revenue by purchasing cloud services from NVIDIA's customers (10-K FY2026). See [OpenAI](../entities/openai.md).
- Business-model risk flagged every year: the services "may not be successful and will take time, resources and investment."
