# Colette M. Kress

Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of [NVIDIA](nvidia.md), joined 2013 (10-K FY2022).

- Age 54 as of March 2022; **58** as of the FY2026 10-K (Feb 2026).
- Career: Cisco Systems 2010–2013 (SVP & CFO, Business Technology and Operations Finance); Microsoft 1997–2010 (from 2006, CFO of the Server & Tools division); eight years at Texas Instruments before that in finance roles.
- Education: B.S. Finance, University of Arizona; M.B.A., Southern Methodist University.
