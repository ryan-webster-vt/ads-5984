# Mellanox Technologies, Ltd.

Supplier of high-performance interconnect products (InfiniBand, Ethernet) for computing, storage and communications. Acquired by [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) to optimize data center workloads across the computing, networking and storage stack (Note 2, 10-K FY2022).

## Acquisition (April 27, 2020)

- Total purchase consideration **$7.13B**: $125.00/share cash for ~56M shares ($7,033M) + cash for equity awards ($16M) + fair value of assumed awards ($85M).
- Allocation: goodwill **$3.43B** (to [Compute & Networking](../concepts/reportable-segments.md)), intangibles **$2.97B** (developed technology $1.64B/5yr, customer relationships $440M/3yr, order backlog $190M, trade names $70M, IPR&D $630M for a next-generation interconnect product), plus net tangible assets.
- FY2021 included a non-recurring **$161M inventory step-up charge** from the acquisition (depressing FY2021 gross margin).
- Revenue attributable to Mellanox was ~10% of FY2021 revenue; operating results consolidated from April 27, 2020.

## Aftermath

- FY2022 was the first full year of networking revenue — a driver of [Data Center](../concepts/data-center.md) growth (+58%).
- Networking scaled with the AI buildout: Data Center networking reached **$12.99B (+51%) in FY2025** (growing, though slower than compute's +162%) and **+142% in FY2026** — the fastest-growing layer that year — on NVLink for GB200/GB300 plus Ethernet/InfiniBand (10-K FY2025, FY2026). See [Networking and interconnect](../concepts/networking.md).
- Mellanox operations give NVIDIA significant engineering, sales-support and manufacturing presence in Israel (10-K FY2022) — ~6,000 employees in the region as of FY2026.
- **China antitrust:** in FY2024 Chinese regulators inquired about NVIDIA's fulfillment of the commitments it made when closing the Mellanox acquisition (10-K FY2024); on **Sept 15, 2025**, China's antitrust regulators issued a **preliminary finding** that NVIDIA's compliance with US export controls (offering degraded products to China) discriminated unfairly against Chinese customers and therefore violated the terms of China's Mellanox approval (10-K FY2026). See [Export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md).
