# Groq, Inc.

An AI-compute company. In **December 2025**, [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) entered a **non-exclusive license agreement** with Groq for its **language processing unit (LPU)** technology and hired certain Groq employees (10-K FY2026).

## The transaction (10-K FY2026)

- **No customer contracts, existing products, or equity interests** were purchased — this is an IP-and-talent license, not an acquisition of the business.
- Total consideration: **$13.0B paid at closing** plus **$4B, inclusive of imputed interest, payable within one year** (in Accrued and Other Current Liabilities) — **$17.0B total**.
- Accounting: **$14.4B goodwill** (attributable to workforce and future development of the licensed technology) and a **$2.5B developed-technology intangible** (cost-to-recreate methodology, five-year useful life). Goodwill recorded in the **Compute & Networking** reporting unit and is tax deductible.
- Pro forma results were not presented (not material).

## Why it matters

- Drives the FY2026 jump in total goodwill from $5.2B to **$20.8B** and the increase in cash used in investing activities (10-K FY2026).
- [Risk factors](../concepts/risk-factors.md) flag that incorporating the licensed technology "requires significant engineering effort and may not occur on expected timelines or at all," and that NVIDIA may not recover the cost.
