# Jen-Hsun (Jensen) Huang

Co-founded [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) in 1993; President, CEO, and Board member since inception (10-K FY2022).

- Age 59 as of March 2022; **63** as of the FY2026 10-K (Feb 2026).
- Career before NVIDIA: LSI Logic 1985–1993 (Director of Coreware, its SoC business unit); AMD 1983–1985 (microprocessor designer).
- Education: B.S.E.E. Oregon State University; M.S.E.E. Stanford University.
- Serves as the chief operating decision maker (CODM) for [segment reporting](../concepts/reportable-segments.md) — reviews segment financial information to assess performance (Note 17, 10-K FY2022).
