# NVIDIA Corporation

Pioneered [accelerated computing](../concepts/accelerated-computing.md). Incorporated in California April 1993, reincorporated in Delaware April 1998. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California (new HQ building completed Feb 2022; NVIDIA owns and leases **~3 million sq ft** of office/building space as of FY2026, up from ~1.76M sq ft reported in FY2022). Fabless — wafers from TSMC and Samsung (10-K FY2022).

- **Fiscal calendar**: 52/53-week year ending the last Sunday in January. FY2026, FY2025, FY2024, FY2023 and FY2022 were all 52-week; FY2021 was 53-week; **FY2027 will be a 53-week year** (10-K FY2026).
- **Stock**: Nasdaq Global Select Market, symbol NVDA; public trading since Jan 22, 1999; **~1,226 registered shareholders as of Feb 20, 2026** (up from ~313 as of March 2022). Ten-for-one split June 2024; 4-for-1 split July 2021.
- **Leadership**: [Jensen Huang](jensen-huang.md) (co-founder, President & CEO), [Colette Kress](colette-kress.md) (EVP & CFO since 2013). Other officers (FY2026 10-K): Ajay K. Puri (71, EVP Worldwide Field Operations), Debora Shoquist (71, EVP Operations), Timothy S. Teter (59, EVP, General Counsel & Secretary) — the same three as in the FY2022 10-K.
- **Auditor**: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP since 2004.
- **Segments**: [Graphics and Compute & Networking](../concepts/reportable-segments.md); four market platforms: [Gaming](../concepts/gaming.md), [Data Center](../concepts/data-center.md), [Professional Visualization](../concepts/professional-visualization.md), [Automotive](../concepts/automotive.md), plus OEM & Other.
- **Employees**: ~42,000 in 38 countries as of Jan 25, 2026 (31,000 in R&D; turnover 3.7%; >80% technical, >50% advanced degrees) (10-K FY2026). Prior: ~36,000 in 38 countries as of Jan 26, 2025 (27,100 R&D; turnover 2.5%) (10-K FY2025); ~29,600 in 36 countries as of Jan 28, 2024 (22,200 R&D; turnover 2.7%) (10-K FY2024); 26,196 in 35 countries as of Jan 29, 2023 (19,532 R&D; turnover 5.3%) (10-K FY2023); 22,473 in 32 countries as of Jan 30, 2022 (16,242 R&D; turnover 4.9%).
- **Cumulative R&D**: over **$76.7B** invested since inception as of FY2026 (10-K FY2026). Prior markers: >$58.2B (FY2025), >$45.3B (FY2024), >$29B (FY2022).
- **Ten-for-one stock split** effective June 2024 (authorized shares 8.0B → 80.0B); prior-period per-share amounts retroactively adjusted (10-K FY2025). Earlier 4-for-1 split July 2021.

## Balance sheet & capital structure (10-K FY2022 → FY2026)

- **Senior notes:** $11.0B FY2022–FY2023 → **$9.7B FY2024** (0.309% notes due 2023 repaid) → **$8.5B FY2025–FY2026** (0.584% notes due 2024 repaid). Maturities out to 2060; unsecured, make-whole redemption; covenants non-financial and complied with. FY2024 face maturities $1.25B (≤1yr), $2.25B (1–5yr), $2.75B (5–10yr), $3.5B (>10yr).
- **Commercial paper:** $575M program unused FY2022–FY2025; **increased to $25.0B in Jan 2026** (still none outstanding) (10-K FY2026).
- **Goodwill:** $4.37B FY2023 → **$4.4B FY2024** → **$5.2B FY2025** (+$758M acquisitions) → **$20.8B FY2026** (Compute & Networking $20.5B; Graphics $370M) — the FY2026 jump is the **Groq IP license ($14.4B goodwill + $2.5B developed technology)**. No impairments.
- **Buybacks:** $7.08B cumulative through FY2022; FY2023 $10.04B; FY2024 $9.7B (new $25B authorization Aug 2023); FY2025 **$34.0B** (new $50B authorization Aug 2024); FY2026 **$40.4B** (new $60B authorization Aug 2025); **$58.5B remaining** as of Jan 25, 2026.
- **Dividends:** $399M (FY2022), $398M (FY2023), $395M (FY2024), $834M (FY2025), $974M (FY2026).
- **Supply/capacity commitments:** $10.3B (FY2022) → $16.1B (FY2024) → $30.8B (FY2025) → **$95.2B (FY2026)**, nearly all payable through FY2027; plus FY2026 cloud-service commitments $27B and investment commitments $11.4B.
- 2000 agreement with Microsoft (Xbox chips) gives Microsoft first/last rights of refusal if anyone offers to buy ≥30% of NVIDIA stock.

## Concentrations (10-K FY2022 → FY2026)

- **Revenue outside the US:** 84% (FY2022), 69% (FY2023), 56% (FY2024), 53% (FY2025) — then the FY2026 10-K **recast** geographic revenue to *customer-headquarters* basis and reports 31% (restated: 41% FY2025, 48% FY2024). See [Export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md).
- **Customer concentration:** none ≥10% FY2021–FY2023. FY2024 first "Customer A" at 13% (+ an indirect customer ~19%). FY2025: three direct customers at 12%/11%/11% plus an indirect customer ≥10%. FY2026: **one direct customer 22% and another 14%** (letters dropped; prior-year series restated to 12%/11%/11%). All primarily Compute & Networking.
- **Receivables:** two customers 24%/11% (FY2024), 17%/16% (FY2025); three customers 25%/18%/13% (FY2026).
- Engineering, sales-support and manufacturing in Israel ([Mellanox](mellanox.md)); manufacturing concentrated in Asia (Taiwan, China, Hong Kong, Korea) with FY2026 plans to expand US/Latin America. FY2023: direct sales to Russia stopped and operations closed.
- **China:** 14% of FY2024 Data Center revenue (down from 19% FY2023); by FY2026 NVIDIA reports being effectively foreclosed from China's data center compute market.
