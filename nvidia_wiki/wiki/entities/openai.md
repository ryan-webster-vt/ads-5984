# OpenAI

An AI research and deployment company. In its FY2026 10-K, [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) discloses that it is **"finalizing an investment and partnership agreement with OpenAI,"** with **no assurance** that agreement will be entered into or a transaction completed (10-K FY2026).

## What the filing says

- The disclosure appears twice: as a risk factor under acquisitions/investments, and in MD&A under expected ecosystem investing (10-K FY2026).
- Separately, the FY2026 10-K states that "one AI research and deployment company contributed to a meaningful amount of our revenue purchasing cloud services from our customers" (no name given) — an indirect-demand concentration theme.

## Why it matters

- Illustrates NVIDIA's shift toward **investing in its own demand ecosystem** (FY2026 invested $17.5B in private companies and infrastructure funds; provided $3.5B of land/power/shell guarantees).
- [Risk factors](../concepts/risk-factors.md) flag counterparty and commercial-arrangement risk from such investments.
