# NVIDIA 10-K FY2026

Annual report on Form 10-K for the 52-week fiscal year ended **January 25, 2026**, signed **February 25, 2026**. Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP (since 2004); internal controls effective. Critical audit matter: valuation of inventories and excess purchase commitments. No restatement or error correction. FY2027 will be a 53-week year.

## What it is

Growth continues at scale but margins compress: **revenue $215.9B (+65%)**, yet gross margin falls to **71.1%** as the model transitions from Hopper HGX systems to **Blackwell full-scale data-center solutions** and absorbs a **$4.5B H20 charge**. Two segments remain, but their **definitions are narrowed this year** ([reportable segments](../concepts/reportable-segments.md)) and geographic revenue is **recast to customer-headquarters basis**. The company describes itself as **effectively foreclosed from China's data center market**.

- **By market platform (dollar figures not recoverable; YoY from narrative):** Data Center **+68%** (compute +59%, networking +142% on NVLink for GB200/GB300 plus Ethernet/InfiniBand); Gaming **+41%** (Blackwell demand; supply constraints expected to be a headwind into FY2027); Professional Visualization **+70%** (Blackwell + DGX Spark); Automotive **+39%**; OEM & Other not disclosed.
- **Gross margin** 71.1%, down 3.9 pts — Hopper HGX → Blackwell full-scale mix and the **$4.5B Q1 FY2026 H20 charge**. Inventory effect −2.6%. Inventory provisions $4.0B; total inventory + purchase-obligation provisions $7.2B; inventory balance $21.4B.
- **Opex** $23.08B (+41%). A **$711M valuation-allowance release** and investment gains (incl. Intel common stock) partly offset tax. Cost of revenue includes tariffs.
- **Groq:** December 2025 non-exclusive **IP license** — no products/equity acquired — **$14.4B goodwill + $2.5B developed technology**, $13.0B paid at closing plus $4B payable within one year (Compute & Networking).
- **Capital:** $62.56B cash + securities; $8.5B notes; **$60B added to buyback authorization** Aug 2025; FY2026 repurchased 282M shares / $40.4B; $58.5B remaining; $974M dividends. **Commercial paper program raised from $575M to $25.0B** (none outstanding). Supply/capacity commitments **$95.2B** (vs $30.8B) — nearly all payable through FY2027; cloud-service commitments $27B; investment commitments $11.4B. $17.5B invested in private companies/infrastructure funds; $3.5B land/power/shell guarantees.
- **Concentration:** 31% of revenue from customers headquartered outside the US (recast; prior basis 41%/48%). **One direct customer 22% and another 14% of revenue** (Compute & Networking); prior-year series restated to 12%/11%/11%. Three customers = 25%, 18%, 13% of receivables. No Customer A/B letters used this year; an "AI research and deployment company" is estimated to contribute meaningfully via cloud services.
- **Export controls ([detail](../concepts/export-controls.md)):** April 2025 H20 license requirement → **$4.5B charge**; Aug 2025 H20 licenses granted (~$60M revenue, USG expected 15% share, not codified); **Feb 2026 H200 license** (small volumes, US inspection + **25% import tariff**); AI Diffusion IFR rescinded May 2025, replacement rule uncertain; GAIN AI Act passed Senate Oct 2025; **effectively foreclosed from China data center compute**; competitors' ecosystems benefit.
- **China antitrust:** Sept 15, 2025 preliminary finding by China's antitrust regulators that compliance with US export controls (degraded products) violated the terms of the **Mellanox** acquisition approval.
- **Legal:** securities class action remanded to district court; derivative suits stayed; broad antitrust requests (EU/US/UK/China/South Korea); **IRS examining FY2023–FY2024**.
- **Roadmap:** **Blackwell Ultra / GB300** shipped Q2 FY2026; **Rubin** unveiled, production 2H FY2027 (~10x cost-per-token improvement vs Blackwell); NVLink Fusion; Nemotron and Cosmos models; physical AI/robotics platform; expanding US and Latin America manufacturing.
