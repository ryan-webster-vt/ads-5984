# NVIDIA 10-K FY2022

Annual report on Form 10-K for the 52-week fiscal year ended **January 30, 2022**, signed **March 17, 2022**. Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP (auditor since 2004). This is the baseline filing for this wiki.

## What it is

NVIDIA's own account of its [accelerated computing](../concepts/accelerated-computing.md) platform strategy, reported in two segments — Graphics and Compute & Networking — across four market platforms ([Gaming](../concepts/gaming.md), [Data Center](../concepts/data-center.md), [Professional Visualization](../concepts/professional-visualization.md), [Automotive](../concepts/automotive.md)) plus OEM & Other.

## Key facts (10-K FY2022)

| Metric | FY2022 | vs FY2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $26.91B | +61% |
| Gross margin | 64.9% | 62.3% |
| Operating income | $10.04B | +122% |
| Net income | $9.75B | +125% |
| Diluted EPS | $3.85 | +123% |
| Cash + marketable securities | $21.21B | $11.56B |
| Notes outstanding | $11.00B | — |
| Effective tax rate | 1.9% | 1.7% |
| Employees | 22,473 (32 countries) | — |

- By market platform: Gaming +61%, Data Center +58% (first full year of [Mellanox](../entities/mellanox.md) networking), Professional Visualization +100%, Automotive +6%, OEM & Other +84% on [CMP crypto-mining revenue of $550M](../concepts/cryptocurrency-exposure.md).
- [Arm acquisition terminated](../concepts/arm-acquisition.md) Feb 8, 2022; $1.36B write-off signaled for Q1 FY2023.
- Supply-constrained year with 12+ month lead times; $1.87B supply prepayments; $10.3B total purchase commitments ([supply chain](../concepts/supply-chain.md)).
- 84% of revenue from outside the US; no customer ≥10% of revenue; two customers = 22% of accounts receivable.
- Capital returns: $399M dividends; **no buybacks in FY2022** ($7.24B authorization remaining through Dec 2022); 4-for-1 stock split July 2021.
- Feb 23, 2022 [cybersecurity incident](../concepts/risk-factors.md) (source code stolen and leaked); Russia sales ceased after the Ukraine invasion ([export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md)).
- Roadmap markers: Grace CPU unveiled, BlueField DPUs + DOCA, Omniverse Enterprise GA, DRIVE Atlan announced, Jetson AGX Orin, Quantum-2 400Gbps networking, Earth-2 climate supercomputer.
