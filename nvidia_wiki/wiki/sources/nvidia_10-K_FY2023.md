# NVIDIA 10-K FY2023

Annual report on Form 10-K for the 52-week fiscal year ended **January 29, 2023**, signed **February 24, 2023**. Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP (auditor since 2004); internal controls effective; ERP upgrade's consolidated financial-reporting phase completed Q2 FY2023.

## What it is

The [accelerated computing](../concepts/accelerated-computing.md) story pivots to **generative AI and large language models**; same two segments (Graphics; Compute & Networking) and four market platforms ([Gaming](../concepts/gaming.md), [Data Center](../concepts/data-center.md), [Professional Visualization](../concepts/professional-visualization.md), [Automotive](../concepts/automotive.md)) plus OEM & Other. New this year: [NVIDIA AI cloud services](../concepts/ai-cloud-services.md) (DGX Cloud, pretrained models) and concrete US export controls on A100/H100 ([export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md)).

## Key facts (10-K FY2023)

| Metric | FY2023 | vs FY2022 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $26.97B | flat |
| Gross margin | 56.9% | 64.9% |
| Income tax | $187M benefit | $189M expense |
| Effective tax rate | −4.5% (benefit) | 1.9% |
| Cash + marketable securities | $13.30B | $21.21B |
| Notes outstanding | $11.00B | $11.00B |
| Capital returned | $10.44B ($10.04B buybacks + $398M dividends) | $399M dividends only |
| Employees | 26,196 (35 countries) | 22,473 (32 countries) |

> Net income and EPS are **not recoverable** from this raw conversion (income statement rendered as `[TABLE]`); the tax disclosures imply income before tax ≈ $4.2B and net income ≈ $4.3B (derived, approximate).

- By market platform: Data Center **$15.01B, +41%**; Gaming **$9.07B, −27%**; Professional Visualization **$1.54B, −27%**; Automotive **$903M, +60%**; OEM & Other −61% ([CMP nominal vs $550M](../concepts/cryptocurrency-exposure.md)).
- Gross margin −8.0 pts on **$2.17B inventory charges** ($1.04B on hand + $1.13B purchase obligations), mostly excess Ampere Gaming/Data Center product vs demand expectations, particularly China; net GM impact −7.5 pts ([supply chain](../concepts/supply-chain.md)).
- [Arm termination charge of $1.35B](../concepts/arm-acquisition.md) recorded in operating expenses (vs $1.36B estimated in the FY2022 10-K); operating expenses +50%.
- Operating expenses also reflect higher compensation, data center infrastructure, engineering development.
- [AI cloud services](../concepts/ai-cloud-services.md): DGX Cloud + customizable pretrained models (NeMo LLM, BioNeMo LLM); **multi-year cloud service agreements with CSPs signed 2H FY2023** ($2.23B of cloud commitments inside purchase obligations).
- Q3 FY2023 [US export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md): license requirements for A100, H100, DGX systems, A100X to China (incl. HK/Macau) and Russia; **A800** offered as an alternative product; certain operations transitioned out of China.
- 69% of revenue billed outside the US (vs 84%), driven by China and Taiwan declines; no customer ≥10% of revenue; two customers = 14% and 11% of accounts receivable.
- Buybacks: 63M shares for $10.04B; $17.12B cumulative since inception; $7.23B authorization remaining through Dec 2023. $1.25B of notes due FY2024; $575M commercial paper program unused.
- $122M warranty charge in Q2 FY2023 (third-party component defect in certain Data Center products); ~$70M favorable recovery in Q3. Warranty liabilities $82M vs $46M.
- Roadmap markers: Hopper architecture / H100 ramp; Ada Lovelace / RTX 4090-4070 Ti, DLSS 3; Spectrum-4 400Gbps; Quantum-2 shipping; OVX server; DRIVE Orin production, DRIVE Thor announced; Omniverse Cloud and Avatar Cloud Engine.
- Mellanox: $630M IPR&D intangible began amortizing Q1 FY2023; amortization expense $699M (FY2023) vs $563M (FY2022).
- Tax: valuation allowance $1.48B (vs $907M); TCJA R&D capitalization began FY2023; IRS examining FY2018–2019; unrecognized tax benefits $1.02B.
- Accounting-estimate change effective FY2024: server/storage/network useful lives 3 → 4–5 yrs, assembly/test 5 → 7 yrs (~+$133M FY2024 operating income from lower depreciation).
- Securities class action: plaintiffs' appeal argued at the Ninth Circuit May 10, 2022 — still pending; derivative suits stayed.
