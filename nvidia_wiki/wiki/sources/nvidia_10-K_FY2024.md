# NVIDIA 10-K FY2024

Annual report on Form 10-K for the 52-week fiscal year ended **January 28, 2024**, signed **February 21, 2024**. Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP (since 2004); internal controls over financial reporting effective. No restatement or error correction (cover-page boxes unchecked).

## What it is

The year the [accelerated computing](../concepts/accelerated-computing.md) thesis converts into a step-change in results: demand for generative AI and large language models drove **Data Center revenue +217%**, making NVIDIA "a full-stack computing infrastructure company with data-center-scale offerings." Same two segments ([Compute & Networking; Graphics](../concepts/reportable-segments.md)) and four market platforms. [Arm](../concepts/arm-acquisition.md) is now a closed chapter; [export controls](../concepts/export-controls.md) and antitrust scrutiny move to the foreground.

## Key facts (10-K FY2024)

| Metric | FY2024 | vs FY2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $60.92B | +126% |
| Gross margin | 72.7% | 56.9% |
| Operating income | $32.97B | +681% |
| Net income | $29.76B | +581% |
| Diluted EPS (as filed) | $11.93 | +586% |
| Effective tax rate | 12.0% expense | −4.5% benefit |
| Cash + marketable securities | $25.98B | $13.30B |
| Senior notes outstanding | $9.7B | $11.0B |
| Capital returned | $10.1B ($9.7B buybacks + $395M dividends) | $10.44B |
| Employees | ~29,600 (36 countries) | 26,196 (35) |

- **By market platform:** Data Center **$47.525B +217%** (compute +244%, networking +133%); Gaming **$10.447B +15%** (channel normalization); Professional Visualization **$1.553B +1%**; Automotive **$1.091B +21%**; OEM & Other **$306M** (down ~33%, computed). ~40% of Data Center revenue was AI inference; in Q4 large cloud providers were >half of Data Center revenue.
- **Gross margin** rose to 72.7% on Data Center mix and lower net inventory provisions; inventory effect was −2.7% (FY2024) vs −7.5% (FY2023). Inventory provisions $774M; total inventory + excess-purchase-obligation provisions $2.2B.
- **Opex** +2% to $11.33B (R&D $8.675B; SG&A $2.654B). FY2023's **$1.353B Arm termination charge** was absent. IRS audit of FY2018–2019 resolved → **$145M non-cash net tax benefit**.
- **Useful-life change** (effective FY2024, disclosed in FY2023 filing): benefit of $135M operating income / $114M net income / $0.05 per share.
- **Concentration:** 56% of revenue outside the US (vs 69%). **First customer ≥10%** in the modern era — "Customer A" 13% of revenue, plus an indirect customer ~19% (Compute & Networking). Two customers = 24% and 11% of receivables. China fell to 14% of Data Center revenue (from 19%).
- **Capital:** $25.98B cash + securities; $9.7B notes (repaid 0.309% notes due 2023); **$25B new buyback authorization** Aug 2023; FY2024 repurchases 21M shares / $9.7B; $22.5B remaining. No commercial paper. Supply/capacity obligations $16.1B; total purchase commitments $20.7B, incl. $3.5B multi-year cloud agreements.
- **Export controls:** Oct 2023 rules imposing licenses on A100/A800/H100/H800/L4/L40/L40S/RTX 4090 to China and Country Groups D1/D4/D5; **no China licenses received**; alternatives shipping in small volumes; some testing/validation/supply moved out of China and Hong Kong.
- **Antitrust:** increased regulatory interest in NVIDIA's AI position — French Competition Authority inquiry; information requests from EU, UK and China (incl. Mellanox-commitment questions).
- **Roadmap:** Hopper/H100 ramp; **Grace CPU and Grace Hopper Superchips** began shipping Q3 FY2024; **Spectrum-X** Ethernet for AI announced; DGX Cloud + AI Foundations; RTX 40 SUPER; Omniverse Cloud on Azure; MediaTek automotive partnership. **Blackwell not yet mentioned** (announced after this filing).
