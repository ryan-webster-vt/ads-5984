# NVIDIA 10-K FY2025

Annual report on Form 10-K for the 52-week fiscal year ended **January 26, 2025**, signed **February 26, 2025**. Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP (since 2004); internal controls effective. Critical audit matter: valuation of inventories and excess purchase commitments. No restatement or error correction.

## What it is

Another doubling year: **revenue $130.5B (+114%)** as [Data Center](../concepts/data-center.md) reached **$115.2B**, over 88% of the company. The **Blackwell architecture** shipped in Q4, extending the annual data-center cadence begun with Hopper. A ten-for-one stock split (June 2024) restated all per-share data. Geopolitics dominates the risk section: the January 2025 **"AI Diffusion" interim final rule** would impose a worldwide licensing regime.

- **By market platform (Note 16):** Data Center **$115.186B +142%** — compute $102.196B (+162%), networking $12.990B (+51%, Spectrum-X/Ethernet for AI); Gaming **$11.350B +9%**; Professional Visualization **$1.878B +21%**; Automotive **$1.694B +55%**; OEM & Other **$389M +27%**.
- **Gross margin** 75.0%, up on Data Center mix, despite a Q2 FY2025 charge for **inventory provisions for low-yielding Blackwell material**; inventory effect −2.3%. Inventory provisions $1.6B; total inventory + purchase-obligation provisions $3.7B. Inventory balance $10.08B (from $5.28B).
- **Opex** $16.41B (+45%): R&D $12.914B; SG&A $3.491B; drivers include +100% compute/infrastructure and +234% engineering development for new products.
- **Stock split:** ten-for-one, June 2024 (authorized shares 8.0B → 80.0B); prior periods retroactively adjusted. Shares outstanding 24.4B.
- **Capital:** $43.21B cash + securities; $8.5B notes (repaid 0.584% notes due 2024); **$50B added to buyback authorization** Aug 2024; FY2025 repurchased 310M shares / $34.0B; $38.7B remaining; $834M dividends. Supply/capacity obligations $30.8B; total purchase commitments $45.1B incl. $10.9B multi-year cloud agreements; prepaid supply/capacity $5.0B total.
- **Concentration:** 53% of revenue outside the US. **Three direct customers ≥10%** (12%, 11%, 11%) plus an indirect customer ≥10% (all Compute & Networking). Two customers = 17% and 16% of receivables. Geographic table lists Singapore $23.68B (18%) — but shipments to Singapore <2%, customers centralize invoicing there.
- **Export controls ([detail](../concepts/export-controls.md)):** recap of Aug 2022/Jul 2023/Oct 2023 rules; **AI Diffusion IFR (Jan 15, 2025)** tiered worldwide licensing (Tier 2 = 150+ countries), UVEU/NVEU; covered products incl. A100/H100/H200/B100/B200/GB200/L40S/RTX 6000 Ada; no China licenses for restricted products.
- **Antitrust:** broadened competition-regulator requests — French Competition Authority plus EU, US, UK, China, South Korea — re GPU sales, supply allocation, foundation models and investments.
- **Securities class action:** Supreme Court granted cert (June 2024), then dismissed the writ as improvidently granted (Dec 11, 2024); Ninth Circuit judgment effective Feb 20, 2025, **remanded to district court**.
- **Roadmap:** **Blackwell launched and shipping** (GB200 NVL72/NVL36, B200); Hopper demand drove the full year; new **annual architecture cadence**; **Grace** CPU in data center platform; GeForce RTX 50 Series (Blackwell); NVIDIA AI Enterprise with **NIM**, NeMo, AI Blueprints; DGX Cloud; Earth-2.
